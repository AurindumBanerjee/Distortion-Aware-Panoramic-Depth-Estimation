# Polar corrections for panoramic depth: results write-up (4 Nov 2026)

All statistics are cluster-robust (scenes resampled by house / environment / scene) and come from
`SUMMARY_20261104_122025.md`, which uses the corrected PanoX grouping (8 environments, not 80 groups).
Gain = median relative AbsRel reduction in the 75-90 deg band against the model's own plain-ERP baseline
(positive = better), shown with a 95% cluster-bootstrap CI and a cluster Wilcoxon p. With 8 clusters the smallest
possible p is about 0.008; with 3 clusters it is 0.25, so Holo outdoor can never reach significance.
Earlier summaries (3 Nov and the 4 Nov 03:21 one) used the wrong PanoX grouping and were moved to `to_delete`.

## Conclusions

1. **Re-centring the pole (Method 2) helps MiDaS only on rendered Structured3D.** Rotation-only gains +16% [+7, +26] on S3
   (`align-all`) and +55% under the strict protocol. On PanoX it is -136% [-172, -27] (8 environments, p 0.023) and on
   real indoor Holo360D with 12 scenes it is -58% [-85, -30]. A real-data claim fails the pre-registered rule
   (cluster CI excludes 0 with at least 12 indoor clusters, yaw control near 0).
2. **Dilation contributes nothing.** Rotation-only matches or beats the 4-pass version on S3 (+16.1% vs +13.7%) and both fail elsewhere.
3. **The mechanism is a decorrelated ensemble, not a better view of the pole.** A single rotated pass is on its own worse at the
   pole on S3 (intrinsic gain -23%, `DIAGNOSE_s3.md`), but averaging it with the baseline gains because the errors are
   uncorrelated; the yaw control (same number of passes, same distortion) gains 0%.
4. **None of the add-ons rescues it.** The fit guard shrinks the losses (PanoX -136% to -85%, Holo indoor -58% to -32%) but never
   makes them positive; its warning signal is informative only on S3 (AUC 0.65; PanoX 0.61, Holo 0.52). Joint re-anchoring and
   consensus do not help either.
5. **Method 1 / Tangent depend on the alignment fit.** The exact 3-term tangent alignment turns S3 from +17.6% / +15.2%
   (old 2-term fit) into -3.7% / -5.7%. The 3-term fit is the correct one, so report it as primary and the old fit as a
   sensitivity check: the earlier S3 gain was largely an alignment artefact.
6. **The planar prior is a modest, protocol-dependent gain on PanoX only.** `align-equatorial`: +15.7% [+12.7, +33.1], p 0.008;
   `align-all`: +6.3% [+0.4, +34.0], p 0.078. It does nothing on S3 and hurts Holo indoor under `align-all` (-11.8%, p 0.007). It is
   the only method that improves a dataset other than S3. Why it fails on Holo indoor is open (acceptance logging added, re-run queued).
7. **2026 panoramic models do not have the polar deficit.** DA360's 75-90 AbsRel is 0.011 (S3), 0.020 (PanoX), 0.056 (Holo indoor),
   at or below its global error; DAP is 0.025 on S3. Rotation / tangent / planar corrections hurt or do nothing; the only consistent
   small gain is the plain yaw ensemble (DAP S3 +1.7%, DA2 S3 +3.8%). DA360 was trained on Structured3D, so its S3 numbers are in-domain.

## Caveats
- **Holo outdoor has 3 scenes (3 clusters)**: no p-value there can fall below 0.25; read those columns as direction only.
- **DAP on Holo360D was run on the first 4 indoor / 2 outdoor scenes only** (95 / 61 frames), not the enlarged set.
- **The 3-term vs 2-term rows come from different run dates**; the 2-term fit is only available for S3 and PanoX.
- **Gains are medians of per-frame relative changes**, which can differ from the change in the median error; read them with the win rates in the full summary.
- **Planar prior acceptance rates are not yet logged** for these runs; the re-run that records them is queued behind the shared GPU.
- Synthetic-scene series are excluded (renderer artefacts).
